    Finding a Gestational Surrogate

    Intended parents may find a surrogate through an agency, a multi-provider matching platform, a personal or professional referral, or an independently coordinated search.

    Use the current guide

    Our current, expanded guide covers the main sourcing routes, screening questions, matching considerations and tradeoffs in one place.

    Read: How to Find a Surrogate in 2026

    What does not change by sourcing route

    No sourcing method replaces medical, psychological, legal and insurance review. A candidate should not be treated as a completed match simply because a profile is available or both sides are interested.

    What to compare

    Compare who recruits the candidate, what screening has actually been completed, who coordinates the case, whether the arrangement is exclusive, what fees are refundable, and who is responsible for legal, insurance and medical-clearance steps.
