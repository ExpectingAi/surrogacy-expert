    About This Site

    About Surrogacy.expert

    A practical educational resource for intended parents pursuing gestational surrogacy in the United States.

    Our Mission

    The path to parenthood through surrogacy is one of the most meaningful — and most overwhelming — journeys a person can undertake. Intended parents deserve clear, useful information that helps them understand their options and make informed decisions at every stage.

    Surrogacy.expert was created to make the process easier to understand. We focus on practical questions intended parents ask most often — including costs, matching, legal frameworks, agency selection, IVF, insurance, and the steps from planning through birth.

    What This Site Covers

    • What gestational surrogacy is and how it works
    • The complete step-by-step process from agency selection to birth
    • Detailed cost breakdowns for major journey expenses
    • Legal frameworks in all 50 states and DC
    • Guides for LGBTQ+ families, single parents, and international intended parents
    • How to find, evaluate, and work with a surrogate
    • The IVF and embryo transfer process
    • How to talk to your child about their surrogacy story

    Editorial Standards

    Our goal is to keep content clear, practical, and current. Cost figures, legal information, medical information, and market conditions can change, so readers should verify important decisions with qualified professionals and review the update date shown on relevant guides.

    Important: Surrogacy.expert provides educational information only. Nothing on this site constitutes legal, medical, or financial advice. Always consult a licensed reproductive attorney, qualified medical professional, and financial advisor before making decisions about your surrogacy journey.
