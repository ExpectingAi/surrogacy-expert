Surrogacy cost by state in 2026

The surrogate’s state can move the total budget by tens of thousands of dollars — but compensation is only one part of the difference.

Short answer: In 2026, many US surrogacy journeys fall roughly between $120,000 and $180,000+, with California and parts of the Northeast usually costing more, while several Midwest and Southern markets can be less expensive. Complex journeys can exceed these ranges.

Why California is usually more expensive

California typically combines higher surrogate compensation, higher professional-service costs, strong demand from domestic and international intended parents, and a mature legal ecosystem. That predictability has value, but it often comes at a premium.

Are the cheapest states automatically the best value?

No. The lowest advertised compensation or agency fee does not necessarily produce the lowest total journey cost. Intended parents should compare:

  • Surrogate compensation and allowances
  • Agency fee and rematch policy
  • Insurance exposure
  • Legal process and parentage costs
  • Travel requirements
  • Clinic and monitoring logistics
  • Expected time to match

Why available-surrogate listings can distort cost comparisons

A directory of currently available surrogates is not necessarily representative of the overall market. Candidates with unusually high compensation expectations can remain available longer and therefore become overrepresented in visible inventories. A better benchmark combines public agency compensation schedules, state comparisons, recent program pricing, and actual matching velocity where available.

What states tend to offer the best balance?

For some intended parents, states such as Florida, Nevada, Colorado, Texas, Ohio, Georgia, or other Midwest/Southern markets can offer a better balance between compensation, legal predictability, provider availability, and total cost than California. The right answer still depends on the intended parents’ legal situation, clinic, embryo status, insurance profile, and preferred matching route.
