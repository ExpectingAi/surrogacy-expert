Budget planning

How much should intended parents budget for surrogacy in 2026?

There is no single U.S. price because programs bundle different services and risks. A useful budget starts with the major cost categories, then checks exactly what is included, excluded or contingent.

Short answer: U.S. surrogacy journeys can vary widely and often reach well into six figures once surrogate compensation, professional fees, IVF, legal work, insurance, escrow, travel and contingencies are combined. The safest approach is to build an inclusion-by-inclusion budget rather than rely on one headline total.

Why two similar quotes may not be comparable

One package may include legal, insurance review, rematching and multiple transfers while another excludes them. A lower headline price can therefore create more financial exposure later. Compare inclusions line by line before comparing totals.

Questions to ask before committing

  • Is surrogate compensation included?
  • Are IVF and embryo-transfer costs included?
  • Are both parties' legal fees included?
  • Who pays for insurance review, premiums and out-of-pocket costs?
  • What happens after a failed transfer?
  • What happens if a rematch is needed?
  • Are travel, escrow and lost wages included?
  • Which costs remain the intended parents' responsibility no matter what?

A safer budgeting method

  1. Get a complete written fee schedule.
  2. Label each item as included, excluded, estimated or contingent.
  3. Separate surrogacy costs from donor and embryo-creation costs.
  4. Model at least one failed-transfer or rematch scenario unless the package clearly transfers that risk.
  5. Keep a contingency reserve instead of budgeting to the minimum quoted number.

Why visible surrogate inventory should not set the budget

Currently available profiles can be selection-biased. Candidates with higher compensation expectations or narrower preferences may remain visible longer. For that reason, this site does not use the average compensation of available profiles as a proxy for the overall market.

Methodology
