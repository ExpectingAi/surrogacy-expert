Surrogacy laws in Idaho

Idaho does not currently have a comprehensive gestational-surrogacy statute creating one uniform statewide pathway for agreements and parentage.

What the yellow status means: Idaho's legal path depends more on existing parentage law, court procedure and case-specific facts than in states with a dedicated surrogacy code. Yellow is not a recommendation or a warning against surrogacy.

Legal framework

Because Idaho does not have a comprehensive gestational-surrogacy statute, intended parents should not assume that contract requirements or parentage procedures used in another state automatically apply in Idaho.

Parentage procedure

The availability, timing and form of a parentage order should be confirmed for the specific case with Idaho reproductive counsel. This page does not rank counties or imply that a particular court guarantees a pre-birth order or birth-record result.

Agreement and medical autonomy

The agreement should address compensation and expenses, insurance, legal costs, medical autonomy, termination provisions and dispute procedures. The gestational carrier remains the patient and retains authority over her own medical care.

Family structure

Do not infer eligibility or parentage outcomes from marital status, sexual orientation or geography alone. The applicable law, jurisdiction and facts of the specific arrangement should be reviewed by Idaho counsel.

What intended parents should verify

Before signing an agreement or beginning treatment, confirm the governing law, jurisdiction, parentage strategy, agreement enforceability and expected birth-record procedure with qualified Idaho reproductive counsel.
