Surrogacy by State

Surrogacy laws in Kentucky

Kentucky does not provide a comprehensive modern gestational-surrogacy statute. Parentage, agreement enforceability, compensation, and procedure should therefore be reviewed for the specific arrangement under current Kentucky law.

Framework classification: Limited / fact-dependent. Yellow does not mean surrogacy is prohibited; it means the legal pathway is less comprehensively codified.

Current law, not proposed legislation

Recent Kentucky bills have proposed more detailed surrogacy rules. Proposed legislation should not be presented as current law unless it has been enacted and taken effect. Intended parents should confirm which provisions are actually in force at the time of their agreement.

Parentage procedure

Kentucky does not offer a single statutory gestational-surrogacy pathway comparable to states with a comprehensive surrogacy act. The appropriate parentage filing, timing, jurisdiction, and birth-record procedure should be confirmed with Kentucky reproductive counsel for the specific case.

Agreement review

The agreement should address compensation and expenses, insurance, medical decision-making, legal costs, dispute procedures, and parentage planning. Do not assume that a pre-birth order or birth-certificate outcome is automatic because a written agreement exists.

No county or cross-border ranking

This guide does not rank Kentucky counties or recommend delivery in another state. Any cross-border plan can raise jurisdictional and procedural questions and should be evaluated for the actual parties, residence, treatment location, and expected birth location.
