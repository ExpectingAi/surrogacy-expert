Surrogacy by State

Surrogacy laws in Wyoming

Wyoming does not have a comprehensive gestational-surrogacy statute. Parentage, jurisdiction, agreement enforceability, and birth-record procedure should be reviewed for the specific arrangement under current law.

Framework classification: Limited / fact-dependent. Yellow describes the legal framework, not whether Wyoming is a recommended or unrecommended state.

Legal framework

Wyoming does not provide a single comprehensive statute governing gestational-surrogacy eligibility, agreement requirements, compensation, parentage, and birth records.

Parentage procedure

The appropriate parentage filing, timing, jurisdiction, and required documentation should be confirmed with Wyoming reproductive counsel for the specific case. This guide does not assume that a pre-birth order is routinely available or guaranteed.

Agreement review

The agreement should address compensation and expenses, insurance, medical decision-making, legal costs, dispute procedures, and parentage planning. The surrogate remains the patient and retains authority over her own medical care.

No county, family-structure, or cross-border ranking

This guide does not rank Wyoming counties, assign legal scores by family structure, or recommend delivery in Colorado or another state as a default solution. Any cross-border plan can affect jurisdiction and procedure and must be evaluated for the specific parties and facts.
