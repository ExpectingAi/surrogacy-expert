    Surrogacy Laws in Oklahoma

    Oklahoma has a specific statutory framework for gestational carrier arrangements. The Oklahoma Gestational Agreement Act, enacted in 2019, establishes requirements for gestational agreements and requires court validation.

    Current legal framework

    Oklahoma Statutes Title 10, §§557.1 and following, create the Oklahoma Gestational Agreement Act. The act defines gestational carrier arrangements, intended parents, donors, gestational agreements and related terms. It applies to gestational arrangements in which the carrier does not contribute genetic material to the child.

    Court validation

    A gestational agreement must satisfy the act’s statutory requirements and be validated by a court. Intended parents should therefore treat judicial validation as part of the legal process rather than assuming that a private agreement alone is sufficient.

    Agreement and screening requirements

    The act contains requirements concerning the parties, written agreement, legal representation, medical and psychological evaluation, execution timing and other protections. The exact requirements should be reviewed with Oklahoma reproductive counsel before embryo transfer.

    Parentage planning

    The statute is designed to provide a legal mechanism for recognizing the intended parents in a compliant gestational carrier arrangement. The filing sequence and any birth-record steps should be confirmed with Oklahoma counsel for the specific case and court.

    Oklahoma should not be described as a state with no surrogacy statute. Since 2019, it has had a dedicated Gestational Agreement Act with court-validation requirements.
