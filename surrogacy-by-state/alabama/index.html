    Surrogacy by State

    Surrogacy Laws in Alabama

    Alabama has a Uniform Parentage Act, but the state deliberately omitted the optional surrogacy-agreement article. As a result, Alabama does not currently provide a comprehensive statutory gestational-surrogacy framework comparable to states with dedicated surrogacy acts.

    Current legal framework

    Alabama Code Title 26, Chapter 17 governs parentage and assisted reproduction. Alabama’s version of the Uniform Parentage Act includes assisted-reproduction provisions but omits the optional article specifically addressing surrogacy agreements.

    What that means for a surrogacy journey

    Because there is no comprehensive surrogacy article, intended parents should not assume a standardized statewide gestational-carrier agreement, pre-birth order or birth-record procedure. Parentage planning should be based on current Alabama law and the exact facts of the case.

    2026 proposals are not current law unless enacted

    Legislation concerning children born through surrogacy has been introduced in Alabama in 2026. A bill or introduced effective date should not be presented as current law unless the measure has actually completed the legislative process and taken effect.

    Before embryo transfer

    Alabama counsel should confirm the agreement strategy, parentage filing, venue, birth-record procedure and any issues created by the parties’ family configuration or genetic connections before treatment proceeds.
