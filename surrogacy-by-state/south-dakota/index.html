Surrogacy by State

Surrogacy laws in South Dakota

South Dakota does not have a comprehensive gestational-surrogacy statute. Parentage, jurisdiction, agreement enforceability, and birth-record procedure should be reviewed for the specific arrangement under current law.

Framework classification: Limited / fact-dependent. Yellow describes the level of statutory detail; it is not a statement that surrogacy is prohibited.

Legal framework

South Dakota does not provide a single comprehensive statutory pathway governing gestational-surrogacy eligibility, agreement requirements, compensation, parentage, and birth records. The absence of a comprehensive statute should not be converted into a blanket prediction about court outcomes.

Parentage procedure

The appropriate parentage filing, timing, jurisdiction, and documentation should be confirmed for the specific parties and expected birth location with South Dakota reproductive counsel. This guide does not assume a pre-birth order is available or unavailable in every case.

Agreement review

The agreement should address compensation and expenses, insurance, medical decision-making, legal costs, dispute procedures, and parentage planning. Separate counsel for the surrogate and intended parents helps ensure each party's interests are reviewed independently.

No cross-border or family-structure ranking

This guide does not characterize South Dakota as categorically nonviable for LGBTQ+ families and does not recommend delivery in Minnesota, Nebraska, or another state as a default solution. Any cross-border plan can affect jurisdiction and procedure and should be evaluated for the actual case.
