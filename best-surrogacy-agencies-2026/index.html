Best surrogacy agencies in 2026: how to compare them

“Best” is not a universal ranking. For intended parents, the better question is which agency has the candidate access, screening standards, contract terms, financial transparency and journey support that fit your circumstances.

Short answer: Do not choose an agency from a top-10 list alone. Compare actual candidate access, recent recruitment activity, screening stage, fee inclusions, rematch terms, exclusivity, escrow structure, insurance support and who remains responsible after a match.

The 10 criteria that matter most

  1. Candidate access: Can you see or meaningfully evaluate current candidates before a major non-refundable commitment?
  2. Recruitment pipeline: How many new candidates entered screening recently, and how many are realistically compatible with your criteria?
  3. Screening standards: What is completed before a profile is presented, and what remains for the fertility clinic?
  4. Matching model: Is matching queue-based, compatibility-based, surrogate-choice-driven, or some combination?
  5. Wait-time definition: Does the agency mean time to first profile, mutual match, medical clearance, or legal clearance?
  6. Fees and inclusions: Separate the agency's own fee from surrogate compensation, IVF, legal, insurance, escrow, travel and contingencies.
  7. Rematch policy: What happens financially if screening fails, a match ends, or another match is needed?
  8. Exclusivity: Can you evaluate candidates from other sources while waiting?
  9. Financial safeguards: Understand who holds journey funds, how releases are authorized and which expenses are outside the agency fee.
  10. Case management: Confirm who coordinates legal, clinic, insurance, escrow, pregnancy support and birth planning after the match.

How to compare agency wait-time claims

A single advertised average can hide major differences between families. Ask for the definition of the metric, the period measured, whether withdrawn families are excluded, and what the agency's recent recruitment volume looks like for intended parents with criteria similar to yours.

How to compare agency fees

Do not compare one headline number with another. Build a line-by-line matrix showing what is included, excluded, estimated, contingent and refundable. Our surrogacy agency fees guide explains the major categories to separate.

Should available surrogate profiles affect your choice?

Yes, but only as one signal. Visible profiles can show whether an agency or platform has candidates you may want to consider, but availability is dynamic and a profile is not the same as medical clearance. Read how to evaluate available surrogates.

Agency-first versus surrogate-first

Some intended parents prefer to select one agency first and let that team manage the journey. Others want to understand candidate availability before making a larger commitment. Compare both models in our surrogate-first vs agency-first guide.
